# SUBSENSE anomaly detector model card

**Model artifact:** `ai_engine/models/isolation_forest.joblib` (verify existence and hash using `python tools/artifact_manifest.py`).

## Intended use

Research and software demonstration on the repository's simulated telemetry feature pipeline. It is not a safety instrument, field-validated subsidence predictor, or basis for operational mine decisions.

## Training data and provenance

The evaluation's learned comparators use event-free synthetic normal, baseline-drift, and shifted-normal feature rows generated by `ai_engine/evaluation/run_all.py` and the backend feature stages. The repository summary reported approximately 59,880 simulator-derived rows; this card does not independently certify that count or claim physical training data. Training/test seed families and the confirmatory protocol are described in `docs/evaluation/PROTOCOL.md`. Confirmatory results use a distinct newly frozen seed family and do not tune the trained detector.

## Method and outputs

Isolation Forest is the detector family. Input feature order and transformations are defined by `ai_engine/pipeline/` and the serialized artifact's loader; verify the artifact and current feature contract before reusing it. The adapter converts the model's score convention to an anomaly-oriented score; see `docs/evaluation/PHASE1_CORRECTNESS.md` and adapter tests. Thresholds are implementation constants, not calibrated risk probabilities. The anomaly score is not a probability of subsidence.

## Evaluation and limitations

The 30-seed benchmark is SIMULATION ONLY. It reports mixed results across programmed scenarios, including poor acceleration-only performance and no universal best method. The nuisance false-alarm rate caveat in `docs/evaluation/CONFIRMATORY_ANALYSIS.md` applies. Dropout detection and a defensible long-run false-alarm budget are unsupported. No independent replication, physical recording, mine trial, hardware validation, or operational study exists.

## Failure modes and prohibited use

Potential failure modes include distribution shift, real sensor calibration and mounting effects, missing or delayed messages, unmodeled environmental nuisance, domain-specific event mismatch, unsupported input features, model loading/version incompatibility, and security key compromise. Do not use for life-safety decisions, mine control, evacuation decisions, regulatory claims, or claims of field accuracy without qualified external validation and a validated safety case.
